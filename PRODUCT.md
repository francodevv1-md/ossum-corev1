# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OSSUM COR is used by operational teams in surgical distributors, orthopedics, and healthcare commerce: coordinators, administrative users, sales/collections users, and logistics/stock operators. They work under time pressure around surgical cases, authorizations, remitos, consumption, returns, documentation, invoicing, and collections.

## Product Purpose

OSSUM COR is an operational multi-company ERP centered on the Surgery/Expediente as the canonical entity. Its purpose is to make the surgical circuit traceable, auditable, and actionable from contacts through surgery, budget, preparation, remito, consumption, return, documentation, invoicing, and collection. Success means users can understand case state quickly, complete required operational data with confidence, and avoid hidden business-rule or traceability mistakes.

## Brand Personality

Claro, clínico, operativo. The interface should feel calm, precise, trustworthy, and built for real work rather than decorative presentation.

## Anti-references

- Generic shiny SaaS templates.
- Corporate-blue template aesthetics disconnected from OSSUM COR's current product language.
- Decorative card-heavy UIs that add visual weight without operational meaning.
- Overly playful, glassy, or marketing-led surfaces that reduce trust in clinical/administrative workflows.
- Interfaces where frontend polish hides unclear business state or weak auditability.

## Design Principles

1. **Operational clarity first**: every visual choice should help users understand case state, required next action, or risk.
2. **Clinical restraint**: use calm hierarchy, readable contrast, and precise labels; avoid ornamental noise.
3. **Traceability over decoration**: important domain signals must be explicit, auditable, and visually discoverable.
4. **Progressive depth**: show the summary first, then allow deeper inspection without overwhelming the main workflow.
5. **Respect canonical rules**: UI improvements must not silently change product, business, auth, DB, or multi-company behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target accessible product UI with strong text contrast, keyboard-reachable controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. Do not rely on color alone for critical status, validation, confidence, or workflow state. Preserve Spanish operational language for user-facing OSSUM COR flows unless a specific artifact requires English.
