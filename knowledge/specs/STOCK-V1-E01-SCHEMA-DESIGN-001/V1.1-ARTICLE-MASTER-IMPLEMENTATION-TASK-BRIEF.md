# Task Brief — V1.1 Article Master Implementation

- **Status:** Approved by Franco — implementation completed; migration application remains pending DEV confirmation
- **ID:** `STOCK-V1-ARTICLE-MASTER-IMPLEMENTATION-001`
- **Mode:** Implementation package, gated
- **Scope:** Article catalog identity and cross-module article lookup

## 1. Objective

Implement the validated V1.1 Article Master definition as the canonical catalog for Stock, Recepción, Preparación, supplier document matching, scanning, boxes, Remitos, Consumo, and Devolución.

Saving an article creates catalog identity only. The article must become selectable in Stock, Recepción, and Preparación with stock `0`; no stock movement or physical trace unit is created by article creation.

## 2. Required behavior

### Article identity

- Generate a unique OSSUM article code.
- Store minimum identity data: description, type, brand/manufacturer, family/model or variant, measure, and unit.
- Preserve current Stock / Artículos fields where they do not conflict with the canonical model.
- Keep incomplete non-critical information visible as pending data instead of blocking initial creation.

### Identifier resolution

- Support multiple manufacturer references, GTIN/EAN values, supplier codes, alternative codes, and OSSUM codes.
- Normalize values before comparison.
- Resolve GTIN/EAN uniquely.
- Resolve manufacturer references with manufacturer context when required.
- Resolve supplier codes with supplier context.
- Surface ambiguous matches for explicit user resolution.
- Never silently overwrite or assign an ambiguous mapping.
- Deactivate obsolete identifiers without deleting historical evidence.

### Traceability policy

- Store article requirements for none, lot, lot + expiry, serial, serial + expiry, or lot + serial + expiry.
- Keep lot, serial, and expiry values out of the article identity.
- Treat UDI/GS1 as Scan Core input, not as an article traceability-policy value.
- Apply policy changes prospectively without invalidating existing stock or pending reservations.

### Availability and UX

- Make a saved article immediately searchable/selectable in Stock, Recepción, and Preparación.
- Show stock `0` until a real stock movement exists.
- Extend the existing Stock / Artículos layout, hierarchy, controls, dialogs, tables, density, responsive behavior, and interaction patterns.
- Do not redesign Stock or introduce a parallel visual language.

## 3. Implementation slices

1. **Schema and persistence** — Article, identifier, supplier mapping, traceability policy, company eligibility, indexes, and audit fields according to the approved Stock V1 design.
2. **Server domain service** — create, update, search, identifier resolution, supplier mapping, and identifier deactivation.
3. **Validation and permissions** — centralized validators, company scoping, duplicate/conflict checks, and authorized mutations.
4. **API / lookup contracts** — article CRUD and shared lookup contracts consumed by Stock, Recepción, and Preparación.
5. **Stock / Artículos UI** — adapt the current form and existing screens without visual redesign.
6. **Cross-module selectors** — replace or connect article selection points without implementing Recepción, StockTraceUnit, or Preparación workflows.
7. **Tests and audit** — unit, route/service, identifier-context, availability, permission, and regression coverage.

## 4. Explicit non-goals

- GoodsReceipt behavior or stock intake.
- StockTraceUnit creation or physical stock movements.
- Reservations, surgical preparation, boxes, Remito, Consumo, or Devolución workflows.
- OCR execution or automatic stock entry.
- Camera scanning application.
- Production data migration, backfill, or real-data mutation.
- Auth architecture changes, provider changes, or production permissions.

## 6. Gates and execution order

1. **G-SCHEMA:** approve exact models, fields, relations, constraints, indexes, and ownership of `prisma/schema.prisma`.
2. **G-MIGRATION:** create the migration artifact after schema review; apply only to an explicitly confirmed disposable DEV database.
3. **G-BACKEND:** implement service, validators, permissions, API, and audit behavior.
4. **G-UI:** connect the existing Stock / Artículos experience and lookup consumers.
5. **G-VERIFY:** run focused tests, typecheck, Prisma validation/generate, build if applicable, and browser QA for the article form and selectors.

No gate is implied by this brief. Production, staging, real data, deploy, commit, push, PR, and merge remain outside this authorization.

## 7. Allowed write set after approval

- `prisma/schema.prisma`
- New migration directory under `prisma/migrations/`
- Article-specific files under `src/lib/services/`, `src/lib/validators/`, `src/lib/permissions/`, and `src/app/api/`
- Existing Stock / Artículos UI files under `src/app/stock/`, `src/components/stock/`, and related article selector components
- Focused tests under `src/__tests__/`
- This specification directory for implementation evidence and handoff

Any need to touch Cirugías, Expediente, Auth, shared security, unrelated stock transactions, or another critical file requires scope review before editing.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

1. An authorized user can create an article with minimum identity data and receive a unique OSSUM code.
2. The article is immediately searchable/selectable in Stock, Recepción, and Preparación with stock `0`.
3. Article creation creates no stock movement and no physical trace unit.
4. Multiple identifiers and supplier mappings are supported with contextual resolution.
5. GTIN/EAN uniqueness and manufacturer/supplier context rules are enforced.
6. Ambiguities and conflicts require explicit resolution and are never silently assigned.
7. Article traceability policy is stored independently from physical lot, serial, and expiry data.
8. UDI/GS1 parsing remains a Scan Core concern.
9. Obsolete identifiers retain historical evidence after deactivation.
10. The UI remains a coherent extension of the existing Stock / Artículos experience.
11. Company scoping, permissions, audit, and validation are enforced server-side.
12. Focused regression tests and required quality gates pass.

## 9. Stop and escalate

Stop before editing if:

- the approved Stock design conflicts with the functional definition;
- a decision requires changing company scope, Auth, security, or provider;
- a physical stock, reservation, box, or preparation behavior is required to satisfy the slice;
- a migration would touch real or non-disposable data;
- the allowed write set expands;
- identifier ambiguity cannot be resolved without a new business rule.
